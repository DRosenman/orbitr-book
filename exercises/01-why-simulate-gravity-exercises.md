## Exercises {.unnumbered}

::: {#exr-ch1-three-body}
Kepler's laws describe one body orbiting one other body. List three systems in the real solar system where you would expect them to be noticeably wrong, and say which other body is responsible in each case.
:::

::: {#exr-ch1-step}
The Moon orbits Earth in about 27.3 days. If a simulation steps forward one hour at a time, how many steps does one orbit take? How many if it steps one day at a time? Which do you think is more likely to give a good answer, and why? (Chapter 5 gives a rule of thumb; guess first.)
:::

::: {#exr-ch1-point-mass}
Earth has a radius of about 6,371 km and the Moon orbits at about 384,400 km. The International Space Station orbits at about 420 km above the surface. For which of these two orbits do you think treating Earth as a point mass is a better approximation, and what feature of Earth might matter for the other?
:::
